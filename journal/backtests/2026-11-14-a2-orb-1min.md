## Compared with the 5-min run (11/13)
- 1-min finds a signal every day (23 vs 19). Totals barely move: flat +50% ≈ −$250 both times; 11:00 hold +$740 vs +$750.
- 11/12 flips to a **call** (matches the owner's 7670C read). 11/11 is still a **put** on rules (the owner took calls: their read differs there).
- The 1-min entry is earlier but also catches more fake breaks: 10/22, 10/29 and 10/30 were stopped within minutes.

## Takeaways
1. Win rate ≈ 43%, average loser ≈ −$58. A flat +50% exit loses money; the method's profit is in the big days (11/5, 10/20, 11/9, 11/13).
2. Holding to 11:00 earns the most but gave back a +50% winner on 11/10 (→ −$75). Half-off at +50% with a breakeven stop keeps most of the upside: worst case on a +50% day ≈ +$25.
3. Every losing day except 10/28 was stopped by the midpoint stop. A rule to skip days where the first cross fails twice may help (10/23, 10/26 and 10/30 started with failed crosses); test next.
