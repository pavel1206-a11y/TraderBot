# A2 exit test: 9 months, 5-min SPY, modeled 0DTE contract (owner ask 2026-11-17)

Script: `a2_exits.py` (daily rows in `a2_exits_days.csv`). Data: SPY 5-min bars for 187 sessions, 2026-02-23 to 2026-11-17.

## Setup
- **Entry (A2, 5-min):** the first close beyond the 9:30–9:45 high/low, then the next close still beyond it; entry by 10:25. That gave 142 signals; 125 were traded (17 had no contract at $0.75–1.25 or lacked 10 days of vol history). Stop: range midpoint ∓ $0.10. If a bar touches both the stop and a profit level, the stop is assumed hit first.
- **Contract:** one 0DTE SPY contract, the OTM strike priced closest to $1.00 (within $0.75–1.25).
- **Pricing model:** Black-Scholes, priced in trading time. IV = the prior 10 sessions' realized vol. $0.03 slippage each way.
- **Check against the real fills of 11/4–11/13 (hold-to-11:00 exit):** most days are within about $15. **11/5 is overstated:** model +$1,274 vs real +$842. Read these as relative comparisons; the dollar levels are approximate.

## Results (one contract a day)
| Exit | All 125 | Feb–Jun (63) | Jul–Oct 8 (43) | Oct 9–Nov 17 (19) | Max drawdown | All without the 3 best days |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a. +50% take-profit, else 11:00 (current) | **−$725** (47% win) | −$427 | −$107 | −$190 | −$1,029 | −$888 |
| b. hold to 11:00 | +$816 (38%) | −$574 | −$230 | **+$1,620** | −$1,066 | **−$1,010** |
| c. half at +50%, breakeven stop on the rest, 11:00 | −$643 | −$557 | −$160 | +$74 | −$1,041 | −$1,098 |
| **d. half at +50%, rest trailed 1 bar behind, 11:00** | **−$83** (47%) | **−$317** | **−$1** | +$235 | **−$741** | −$609 |
| e. same as d, rest can run to 12:30 | −$217 | −$210 | −$205 | +$199 | −$918 | −$743 |

The VWAP-side filter (0DTE checklist item 1) **never rejected a signal**: a break past the range is always on the right side of a VWAP that is moving its way. So it adds nothing here.

## Findings
1. **d beats the current rule (a) in every period** (all four periods, +$642 overall) and has the smallest drawdown. **b's profit is one regime:** it lost Feb–Oct (−$804) and made everything in the last 6 weeks, mostly 11/5. Take away the 3 best days and b is the worst exit. **d is the exit change.**
2. **The entry has no proven edge on its own.** Over 9 months, the win rate is about 47% and the average win roughly equals the average loss. **No exit rule is clearly profitable in Feb–Oct.** The recent run is a trending market (10/9–11/17), which A2 suits.
3. The owner's own discretion (reading the "confirmation") is not in the mechanical rule, and it may be where the edge is. The owner's real trades are the test.
4. Next test (proposed, not run, to avoid curve-fitting): one pre-registered **entry** filter that targets trend days, e.g. trade only when the 9:30–9:45 range is narrow relative to the 10-day average (compression → expansion), judged on Feb–Jun and checked on Jul–Nov.
