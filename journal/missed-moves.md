## What keeps happening

1. **Midday squeeze breakout (4 of the last 7 sessions).** Between 12:00 and
   2:00 PM, SPY goes quiet in a tight range for 45 minutes to 2 hours, often
   sitting on a support level, then one 5-min candle breaks the range on 2–8×
   normal volume and SPY runs $2.70–4.60 within an hour. All four were up
   moves. That may be this market's uptrend, so the rule trades both ways and
   needs QQQ to agree.
2. **Morning flush, afternoon recovery.** 9/25, 9/28, 10/1: a sharp drop
   between 10:00 and 11:00 (often on 10:00 data) that was fully or partly
   recovered by the afternoon. The 4-hour pattern (setup D) describes this; it
   needs the 12–4 PM candle to sweep the morning low first.
3. **Gap days trend.** When SPY gaps $4+ and the first 15 minutes hold the
   gap side, the opening-range breakout ran all day (9/21 up, 9/23 down).
4. **Closing selloffs** after 3:30 are out of our window. Not worth chasing.

## Changes made

- Playbook **setup F (midday squeeze breakout)** with measured-move targets.
- Every check now reports the current range edges, so a break can be spotted.
- End-of-day routine adds a short missed-move review to this file every day.
- Real-money phase: put Robinhood price alerts on the range edges so the break
  reaches your phone the moment it happens. Checks alone are too slow: these
  moves run in 15–30 minutes.
